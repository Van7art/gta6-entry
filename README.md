**Porto Verde: Full Throttle — Golden Hour** — an original open-world crime game in the browser. Jax Moreno comes home to Porto Verde after five years away: his brother's garage is gone, the debts are circling, and fixer Lena Cross has work for a driver who remembers every street. Across town, casino kingpin Dante Ferreira owns the docks and half the cops. Drive a living coastal city — Downtown towers, the Playa Dorada beachfront, the Porto Docks harbour and the Barrio Viejo suburbs — with dense traffic that stops at lights, pedestrians with routines who scatter when trouble starts, and arcade car handling with handbrake drifts, crash damage and smoke. Steal any car (sedans, taxis, vans, trucks, sports cars), outrun the PVPD through a 5-star wanted system with the "Air One" police helicopter (searchlight, aerial fire), roadblocks and spike strips, fight Ferreira's crew on foot with a weapon wheel (pistol / SMG / shotgun), and play eight chained story missions — First Gear, Fare Game, Dockside Dash, Street King, Beach Run, The Ferreira Job, Harbour Heist (clear out Ferreira's crew on foot) and Kingpin's Fall (a 10-gate convoy chase against three escorts at four stars of heat) — plus taxi fares, street races, collectible cash coins, health and ammo pickups, a full Cross Motors garage shop (3-stage engine and armour, 9 paint jobs, Sport/Chrome wheels, spoiler — the best stock locked behind respect ranks ROOKIE → STREET KING), a repair/respray garage, a live minimap, save/load with a Continue button, three synthesized WebAudio radio stations (Q or 1-3 to switch, click the station name), pause, and a full HUD. Every city, character, vehicle, name, story, artwork, sound and song is original.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```
