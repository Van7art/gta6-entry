**Porto Verde: Full Throttle** — an original open-world crime game in the browser. Jax Moreno comes home to Porto Verde after five years away: his brother's garage is gone, the debts are circling, and fixer Lena Cross has work for a driver who remembers every street. Across town, casino kingpin Dante Ferreira owns the docks and half the cops. Drive a living coastal city — Downtown towers, the Playa Dorada beachfront, the Porto Docks harbour and the Barrio Viejo suburbs — with dense traffic that stops at lights, pedestrians with routines who scatter when trouble starts, and arcade car handling with handbrake drifts, crash damage and smoke. Steal any car (sedans, taxis, vans, trucks, sports cars), outrun the PVPD through a 5-star wanted system, fight Ferreira's crew on foot, and play six chained story missions — First Gear, Fare Game, Dockside Dash, Street King, Beach Run and The Ferreira Job — plus taxi fares, street races, collectible cash coins, a repair/respray garage, a live minimap, save/load, pause, synthesized WebAudio engine/siren/radio sound and a full HUD. Every city, character, vehicle, name, story, artwork, sound and song is original.
