**Solmar Bay: Streets of Gold** — an original open-world crime game in the browser. Rex Marlowe walks out of the Marina lockup with $150 and one name: Mara Voss, the fixer who runs the Sunset Garage. Across the harbour, Dario Kells owns the docks with an iron fist. Explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Solmar Bay PD through a 5-star wanted system, fight Kells' crew, and play through six chained story missions — First Light, The Courier, Hot Wheels, The Shakedown, Harbor Lights and The Big Score — earning cash and Street Rep along the way. Traffic, pedestrians, a live minimap, collectible gold coins, save/load, pause, synthesized WebAudio sound and music, and a full HUD included. Every city, character, vehicle, name, story, artwork, sound and song is original.
